Residual risk — named, not mitigated

The previous slide is what is controlled. This is what is not, and what each one needs.

No contraindication data exists. There is no allergy, intolerance or preference anywhere in the extract, so “24 of 28 diabetics have no statin” cannot tell a care gap from a documented reason. Needs: that data, before any recommendation is shown as a gap.

Nothing validates the ranking. Every control opposite verifies that a fact is true; none verifies that a priority is, and the product is an ordering. Partly addressed: a transparent score decomposes each patient into named contributions a clinician can edit, and it is computed independently of the agents — so where the two disagree is a specific, short list to put in front of one. Needs: 25 pairwise comparisons, fifteen minutes, not a labelling project.

The model is not pinned. A provider‑side update changes behaviour and nothing re‑runs the eval. Needs: a pinned snapshot and the five‑run eval as a release gate.

Note text is untrusted input. Free text reaches a model in the reconciliation step. Benign in synthetic data; not an assumption that survives production. Needs: treating note content as data, never instruction.

Nothing here recommends a drug or a dose, and no finding is actioned without a human reading the evidence behind it.
